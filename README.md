I am Ryan Duguid, a senior accountant in Newcastle, NSW, Australia and a Provisional CA ANZ Member. I build open source Australian computational accounting tools: 76 browser tax and business calculators, 12 ATO rate tables, 7,430 depreciation effective lives, native Excel LAMBDA functions, month-end review controls and a local MCP server. Not to be confused with the US software product executive of the same name.

## Try an accounting task

- **Run 76 Australian calculators:** [business and individual tax calculators](https://duguid.com.au/tools/business-calculators/) in your browser, with WebMCP support and primary sources visible.
- **Explain profit and cash differences:** a [fictional Newcastle cash-flow case](https://duguid.com.au/examples/profit-vs-cash-flow/), with its source in [OzFPA](https://github.com/ryanduguid/OzFPA).
- **Calculate in Excel:** [Ozzit](https://github.com/ryanduguid/Ozzit) has 165 native Excel LAMBDA functions plus 5 help tables, with a [worked GST example](https://duguid.com.au/tools/ozzit/#worked-example).
- **Review a month-end close:** [Monthly Close Controls](https://duguid.com.au/tools/monthly-close-controls/#worked-example), part of the [Accounting Review Pipeline](https://github.com/ryanduguid/accounting-review-pipeline).

More tools, including a local MCP server for Australian tax calculations, are on the [tools page](https://duguid.com.au/tools/). I also contribute tax skills and guides upstream to [OpenAccountants](https://github.com/openaccountants/openaccountants). This account holds my professional projects. My personal projects, free tools for coding agents and privacy, are on my other account, [yungSNAT](https://github.com/yungSNAT).
